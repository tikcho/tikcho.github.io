GEMI

Find Your Tribe Through Chemistry-First Friend Matching

GEMI uses AI to match you into intimate friend groups of 3-5 people based on personality, interests, and values. No swiping, no awkward 1-on-1. Just authentic connections.

How It Works

  • Profile & Discover: Complete psychological questionnaire, set interests, social battery, verify profile
  • Weekly Matching: Every Sunday 6 PM, AI creates optimal 3-5 person pods
  • Anonymous Trial: Join temporary group chat with compatibility scores (no photos/names). AI suggests ice-breakers.
  • Lock It In: If all vibe → reveal identities & keep pod permanent; if not → re-match next week
  • Stay Connected: AI organizes activities, coordinates plans. Users rate experiences and vouch for members.

Key Features

  • Smart Matching: Our AI-powered matching is far more sophisticated than competitors' basic filters
  • Chemistry-First: No swiping, no photo judgment, just compatibility
  • Anonymous Trial Period: Reduces social anxiety and superficial photo-based judgment
  • 3-5 Person Sweet Spot: Intimate but not awkward (validated by 2 years of research)
  • AI Coordination: Suggests topics and activities; handles scheduling, ice-breakers and booking venues
  • Inner Circle Feature: Add existing friends for status-based coordination (e.g., "X is free for drinks tonight too")

The Problem We Solve

Despite being more connected digitally than ever, roughly 1 in 6 people globally report feeling lonely, especially post-pandemic.

Existing solutions fail: Bumble BFF has swipe fatigue and ghosting. Meetup groups are too large and impersonal. Others have small user bases and no local presence.
