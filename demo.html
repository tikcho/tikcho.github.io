GEMI

A smart social discovery App for real connections.

GEMI matches people into pods of 3–5 based on psychological compatibility, interests, vibes, and social dynamics. No swiping. No random chats. Just genuine connections for people who want to meet the right group.

  • Users register and complete a short questionnaire
  • Profiles are verified to build trust
  • An algorithm analyzes personality, interests, and social fit
  • Matches are made weekly, for example every Sunday evening
